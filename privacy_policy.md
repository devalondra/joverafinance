# Privacy Policy for Jovera Finance

Effective date: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Jovera Finance ("Jovera Finance", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Jovera Finance mobile application (the "App") and related services. This policy is designed to meet Google Play and Apple App Store privacy requirements.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1) Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

### A) Information you provide
- Account information: name, email address, phone number, password.
- Contact and support requests: callback requests, messages, and any details you submit.
- Loan application details: loan type, requested amount, repayment period, nationality, employment or business details, property details (for mortgages), and other information you provide in application forms.
- Documents you upload: passport, Emirates ID, salary certificate, bank statements, Etihad Bureau documents, trade license, memorandum, VAT payment documents, and other supporting files.
- In-app chat content: messages, attachments, and metadata required to deliver the chat.

### B) Information collected automatically
- Device and app information: device type, operating system, app version, language, and IP address.
- Usage data: app interactions, pages/screens accessed, and feature usage to help us improve the App.
- Push notification tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens used to deliver notifications.

## 2) How We Use Your Information

We only use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Process and facilitate loan and mortgage applications and consultations.
- Verify identity and validate documents.
- Communicate with you, including through in-app chat and notifications.
- Provide customer support and respond to requests.
- Improve, maintain, and secure the App and our services.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 3) Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information if you provide us,:
- With banks and financial institutions to evaluate and process your loan applications, with your consent or at your request.
- With service providers who help us operate the App (hosting, cloud storage, communications, push notifications, and security).
- With legal or regulatory authorities when required by law, regulation, or legal process.
- In connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger, acquisition, or sale of assets).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4) Permissions and Device Access

The App may request access to:
- Camera and photo library: to capture or upload documents.
- File storage: to upload documents and attachments.
- Notifications: to send status updates and messages.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without the required permissions.

## 5) Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations. If you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize your data unless we are required to keep it for legal, compliance, or security purposes.

## 6) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 7) Your Choices and Rights

You can:
- Update your profile information in the App.
- Opt out of push notifications through your device settings.
- Request account deletion through the App (Settings > Delete Account) or by contacting us.
- Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

## 8) Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it.

## 9) International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place where required by law.

## 10) Third-Party Services

We use third-party services such as Firebase Authentication and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies.

## 11) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the App and update the effective date.
